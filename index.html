ABOUT THE STUDIO

MORE THAN A DANCE CLASS.

Vishnu's Thunder Dance Studio, founded by Vishnu, is a dynamic platform where energy meets expression, passion meets discipline, and every dancer is encouraged to discover their own rhythm.

We welcome both kids and adults and offer different styles of dance to suit your interests and goals.

Our focus is not only on dance steps, but also on confidence, creativity, discipline and having fun while learning.
